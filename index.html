Adventures in Fire Island

Our trip to Fire Island was the perfect weekend escape. We rode the ferry out from the mainland, walked the wooden boardwalks that cross over the dunes, and spent our evenings watching the sunset over the water. With no cars on the island, everything felt slower and quieter than a typical beach trip.

Highlights included exploring the sand dunes covered in beach grass, walking the boardwalk trails down to the shore, and catching an unforgettable sunset on our last night. It's a great destination for anyone looking to unplug for a few days.
